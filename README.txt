See who is at your door without leaving the couch. This app connects your Reolink video doorbell to Homey over your local network, so a doorbell press, motion, or a person, vehicle, pet, face or package at the door can start your Flows right away. No cloud account is needed.

The doorbell also shows a camera snapshot on its device tile. The app talks to the doorbell directly through ONVIF, so make sure ONVIF is enabled on the doorbell and add it with a local user account of the doorbell.
